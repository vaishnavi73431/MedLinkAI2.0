💧 Feature Deep Dive

Smart Hydration Hero

🧮 Personalized Formula

Base = Weight × 35ml  |  +500ml if Active  |  +1000ml if Athlete  |  ×1.15 if Temp > 30°C

Example: 70kg Active user on hot day → 3.4L (14 glasses)

⏱️ Time Gating

Each glass is locked for 30 minutes after the previous — live countdown shown on the button. Prevents instant cheating.

📸 Camera Verification

At 50% and 100% milestones, users must confirm with a photo of their glass/bottle. Two checkpoints, not every sip.
